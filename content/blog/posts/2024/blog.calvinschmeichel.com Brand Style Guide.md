### Tags

> [!info] Tags
> - `#Post`: For more shorter "Casual" written content about a variety of subjects.
> 	- Could include but not limited to: Updates, thoughts, announcements and more.
> - `#Article`: For more longer "Professional" written content about topics related to security, technology and other related areas.
> 	- Could include but not limited to: career, certifications, current events, stories and more.
> - `#Out_of_Band`: For Articles or Posts that are outside of the typical topics of security, technology and other related areas.
> 	- Could include but not limited to: Creative projects (Photography, Videography, Art, Gaming, Travel and more). But most should be loosely connected to the main areas.
> 	- **Out of Band (OoB)**: "*Is a method of using a separate communication channel*"
