I went to DEF CON 33!

This was my first DEF CON this year and it was a blast! I was able to go with some of my friends from college. We have been talking and planning this trip for about 2 years so it was nice to finally be able to go after graduation last year.

I wanted to share my experiences with the con and what I have learned and tips for attending.

### Day 1 (Line-Con/Badge Day)
The first day of Def Con was awesome, we got in early from our flight, dropped our bags off and went straight to the convention center. The first day is badge day or "Line-Con" as the cool kids say. Today is mostly getting your badge, learning the hall layout and connecting with other people. The halls will be closed today since they are still setting up. This does not mean there is still not events being ran. We ran into a table top exercise event in an un-marked conference room demoing the Meshtastic devices. This was completely by chance! After that we met with some other industry members and went to some after hours networking events.

### Day 2 (Day one of the DEF CON show floor)
Today was mostly and exploring day, we tried to take hot laps around the entire show floor and see all of the booths. We then went and took a look at some of the DEF CON villages. Villages are smaller sub-groups/clubs within the DEC CON community. These can range from red and blue team villages to cloud, hardware hacking, and more. This are really fun places to hang out and meet people with similar niche interests to you in the cybersecurity industry.

After lunch we went to another netwroking event, this time we met multiple content creators such as [Tyler Ramsbey - Hack Smarter](https://www.youtube.com/@TylerRamsbey) and [NetworkChuck](https://www.youtube.com/@NetworkChuck/featured)! It was awesome to meet these people in person and thank them for the hours of free learning content they put out.

We then went to Area 15 for dinner, would highly recommend this part of town if you are looking for places to eat and hangout, they have some wonderful sculptures and fun stores and exibits to explore. Including John Wick's   Car!

After that we went back to the convention center to go see the arcade party, here there was a DJ and real arcade cements to play on. We also saw [John Hammond](https://www.youtube.com/@_JohnHammond) and got to meet [Jack Rhysider (Dark Diaries host)](https://www.youtube.com/@JackRhysider)
 

### Things I learned
##### Wear a fun shirt!
I wore mostly plain colored business causal outfits, but what I saw from the con is that you can where anything from plain clothes to a full suit, all is welcome! But if you were a fun shirt (Graphic Tee, artwork, words, etc) this makes it an easy ice breaker for people to approach you with (Its also a great way for you to approach someone as well and network!)

### Tips
- DEF CON is at the LVCC West Hall (Other halls will be closed, this is the hall by the [Las Vegas Marriott](https://maps.app.goo.gl/PfjJ2QW44gmg7cGT9))
- The Vegas Loop (Tesla taxi car service through The Boring Company tunnel) is free for all attendees with a DEF CON official badge. NOTE: The Vegas Loop is only free for connections to the other convention hall stations, if you want to go to a casino station you will have to buy a normal ticket.
- Show up EARLY to the merch line on day one (Line will be multiple hours long if you don't) and everything will be sold out most likely. Otherwise if you show up early day one when the show floor opens the sometimes have restocks at that time too.
